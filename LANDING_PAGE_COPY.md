\#\# Tone & Voice Guidelines

\#\#\# Brand Voice Attributes  
\- \*\*Transparent\*\*: No hidden fees, clear pricing, honest about what we do/don't do  
\- \*\*Developer-friendly\*\*: Technical but not pretentious, respect the reader's intelligence  
\- \*\*Cost-conscious\*\*: Emphasize savings without being cheap or low-quality  
\- \*\*Reliable\*\*: Professional, trustworthy, enterprise-grade despite lower cost

\#\#\# Do's  
✅ Use specific numbers (70% savings, 99% accuracy, \<300ms response)  
✅ Address pain points directly (expiring credits, high costs, poor docs)  
✅ Show technical credibility (API examples, architecture decisions)  
✅ Be honest about limitations (no email warmup, no mobile app)

\#\#\# Don'ts  
❌ Over-promise or use vague marketing speak ("revolutionary," "game-changing")  
❌ Bash competitors by name (compare features objectively)  
❌ Use excessive exclamation points or hype  
❌ Make absolute claims without data ("best in the world")
